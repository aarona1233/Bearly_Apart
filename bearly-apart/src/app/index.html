    Pairing
    📱 iPhone shortcut

    Send kisses and statuses from your phone. In the Shortcuts app use Get Contents of URL → Method POST, this URL, a header named Tags with one of the values below, and any text as the body. Full walkthrough in the README.

    Advanced

    Panic button: ⌘/Ctrl + Alt + Shift + B hides the bears and snoozes for an hour.
