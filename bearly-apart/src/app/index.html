Hi! Let’s set up Bearly Apart

Little bears will run across your screens when you two text, send kisses, or miss each other. Setup takes about a minute.

Already use Bearly Apart on another computer?

Link this computer

On your other computer, open Bearly Apart → Settings → Your computers → Copy link code, send it to yourself, and paste it here. This computer becomes you too: same name, same bear, connected to the same person. Nothing changes on their side.

Let the bears see your texts

Bearly Apart just updated itself, and macOS wants you to switch it back on once. In the list, switch Bearly Apart off and on again (your Mac asks for your password). That’s all.

Bearly Apart reads your Messages on this Mac to know when your person texts you. It only looks at their messages, never sends anything, and your texts never leave this Mac.

  1. Click Open System Settings.
  2. Find Bearly Apart in the list and switch it on. Your Mac asks for your password.
    Not in the list? Click + at the bottom, then pick Applications → Bearly Apart.
  3. If your Mac offers Quit & Reopen, click it. Setup carries on by itself.

⏳ Waiting for the switch…

Also check Messages → Settings → iMessage → Enable Messages in iCloud is on, so your phone’s texts show up on this Mac.
